## 🔬 Research Interests

> **Brain Network Aging and Resting-State fMRI**  
> I am interested in how functional brain networks change during healthy aging. My current work explores network segregation, integration, and functional connectivity in language, reward, and social networks using resting-state fMRI data.

> **Dance Intervention and Brain Health**  
> I am exploring whether dance-based intervention may support healthy brain aging, with a focus on longitudinal changes in brain function and network organization among older adults.

> **Medical Imaging and Artificial Intelligence**  
> I am learning how to apply machine learning and deep learning to medical imaging tasks, including MRI sequence classification and clinically meaningful image analysis.

---

## 🌱 Long-term Vision

> I hope to become an interdisciplinary researcher who can connect cognitive neuroscience, medical data, and artificial intelligence. My long-term goal is to use rigorous research and practical technical skills to better understand the human brain and contribute to more accessible, trustworthy healthcare technologies.

## 📌 Selected Projects

### 🧠 Brain Network Aging in Older Adults
- Investigating age-related changes in functional network segregation and integration.
- Using rs-fMRI, AAL atlas-based ROIs, functional connectivity matrices, and network analysis.
- Focusing on language, reward, and social brain networks.

### 💃 Dance Intervention and Brain Plasticity
- Studying healthy older adults before and after a dance intervention.
- Exploring whether intervention-related brain changes may support cognitive and emotional well-being.

### 🦵 Knee MRI Sequence Classification
- Building a deep-learning workflow for classifying knee MRI sequences.
- Working with 3D medical images, data cleaning, model inference, and performance evaluation.
